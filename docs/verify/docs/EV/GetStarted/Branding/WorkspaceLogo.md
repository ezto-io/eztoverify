In the initial sign-up process, you have the opportunity to set your **Workspace** logo. However, if you missed this step or wish to update the logo later on, the **Workspace** provides an easy option to make the necessary changes.

## To begin with, 

*1* Navigate to **Workspace** and click on **Settings.**

*2* Click on the **General** tab.

*3* Look for the **Workspace Logo** section.

![Logo](https://images.tango.us/workflows/5db3c080-e6bd-425f-9ae2-11282077f271/steps/d454f7f5-eeec-4514-8a9a-31e9b84248d4/92fefc96-798e-471b-92f2-b35695e9841d.png)

*4* Click on **Change Logo** to initiate the logo update process.

:::note
Ensure that the logo you upload meets the specified requirements: size under **4mb,** resolution under **1024,** and shape as **square**.
:::

*5* After uploading your preferred image, click on **SAVE** to apply the new logo to your **Workspace**.
