It serves as a prominent cloud-based email delivery service that aids businesses in creating, sending, and analyzing email communications with efficiency.

### Prerequisites {#sparkpost-prerequisite}

* You need to update own email provider in your subscription.
* You must have an active [**SparkPost**](https://app.sparkpost.com/join) account.

### Access your SparkPost

* Log in to your [SparkPost](https://login.sparkpost.com/u/login?state=hKFo2SBYUktReTVkUUFMN0tVc3dUWG1QZVN6eExEaXJwdVlqZqFur3VuaXZlcnNhbC1sb2dpbqN0aWTZIEN0bWxNT3d2aEU3aXdUaHprX0hLY3B5MUtwcEVYZW4to2NpZNkgUXR5QVJpOEZoTlpUZ2xCWXdPNWRTeXpzZG5RdmR4Q3k) account.
* As the first step, create a new **API Key.**
*   Click on Configuration > API Keys > Provide a descriptive name to the API, and create the **API Key.** Additionally, select your preferred **Region.**


![Create API Key](https://images.tango.us/workflows/32b0d0a9-db6e-43ce-9169-67c031d15c13/steps/3a408d92-3d69-4ddb-adbd-4139a6b70a59/2af0825d-b337-459c-8e88-fd0de9c8f9e3.png)

* Copy the generated API Key.

### Configure SparkPost

*1* Navigate to **Workspace,** click on **Notification,** and choose **Email.**

*2* Click on `+ADD PROVIDER`

*3* You will be directed to the **New Email Provider** page, where all available providers will be displayed.

![SparkPost](https://images.tango.us/workflows/32b0d0a9-db6e-43ce-9169-67c031d15c13/steps/978daa16-4b91-4427-9543-4e0d05e46660/c5081733-bfe9-40a5-b176-0e5a7805a957.png)

*4* Choose the  **SparkPost.**

*5* You will be taken to the **SparkPost** provider configs page. To obtain the necessary configs, log into **SparkPost.** Refer to the [prerequisites](#sparkpost-prerequisite) on how to do it.

![Configurations](https://images.tango.us/workflows/32b0d0a9-db6e-43ce-9169-67c031d15c13/steps/72505282-6e0c-4e9c-a716-fa438fc95651/7173ab4e-5c01-4f47-89be-8f9458ecedcb.png)

*6* Navigate back to your **Workspace** and input the **API Key.**

*7* After providing the necessary configurations.

*8* Click on `TEST CONFIGURATION` to ensure the configuration looks good.

![Test Configuration](https://images.tango.us/workflows/32b0d0a9-db6e-43ce-9169-67c031d15c13/steps/1a5e39c9-adfa-4a64-aa43-c50fba848554/66457503-e66a-4f48-a77d-00d056dd6ab5.png)

*9* Provide email address and click on **SUBMIT.**

*10* If everything is set up correctly, a test success email will be sent to the email address you provided earlier. Click on the **SAVE** button**.**
