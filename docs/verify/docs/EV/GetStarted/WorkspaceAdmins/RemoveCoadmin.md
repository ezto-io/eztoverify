# Remove Co-Admin

In the lifecycle of managing a **Workspace**, there might come a time when you need to adjust the admin roles, which could involve removing co-admins or to revoke their administrative access from the **Workspace**.

> **Before proceeding with the removal process, it's essential to understand the implications of removing a co-admin will impact their access to workspace settings and management functionalities.**

### To begin with,

*1* Navigate to **Workspace** and click on **Settings**.

*2* Choose **Workspace Admins** from the menu.

In the **Workspace Admins** tab, you'll find a list of current admin, including the account creator (owner).

![Workspace Admins](https://images.tango.us/workflows/85a19880-dd21-4b1c-ab4b-bc12f987dc36/steps/33e47dc3-e2d0-483b-8875-93e09d3e0856/2af1a3ec-2c04-4d99-a313-1a71da69c9d8.png)

### Removing a Co-admin

*1* Identify the co-admin you wish to remove from the list.

*2* Click on the three-dotted option button next to their name.

From the options provided, you'll have the choice to **edit**, **demote**, or **delete** the co-admin.

![Add Admin](https://images.tango.us/workflows/85a19880-dd21-4b1c-ab4b-bc12f987dc36/steps/42ccb91a-89ec-4bfb-979e-0090d78c5e06/52994a4e-156f-4afc-8788-192fd9dd0ee6.png?mark-x=1355&mark-y=309&m64=aHR0cHM6Ly9pbWFnZXMudGFuZ28udXMvc3RhdGljL2JsYW5rLnBuZz9tYXNrPWNvcm5lcnMmYm9yZGVyPTQlMkNEQjMwMzAmdz0zMTImaD0yMTYmZml0PWNyb3AmY29ybmVyLXJhZGl1cz0xMA%3D%3D)

### Demoting the Co-admin

If you wish to revoke only the co-admin's administrative access while retaining their presence in the **Workspace**, choose the **demote** option. Only the **Workspace** creator (owner) can demote a user. For  more information about permissions, see [here.](/verify/docs/EV/GetStarted/WorkspaceAdmins/InviteCoadmin/)

### Deleting the Co-admin

If you wish to completely remove the co-admin from your **Workspace**, click on the **Delete** option.

Upon selecting either of these options, you will be prompted with a confirmation pop-up to confirm your action.
