The **Audit Logs** functions as a comprehensive record of all actions performed outside the **Workspace**, providing detailed information. This includes  email notifications, logins, and other significant events.

This page allows users to view, search, and analyze logs to ensure transparency, accountability, and security within the **Workspace**.

Some of the things you can analyze with these **Logs** are:

* View transaction made by a particular user.

* Search for specific logs by entering relevant details.

* Check the time of every event performed.

> **To access the logs, ensure that the logs are enabled.**

<br/>

###  Enable Logs

*1* Navigate to **Workspace**, and click on **Audit**.

*2* In the **Logs** page,  click on the `SETTINGS` button located at the top right corner, toggle the **Enable** button, and then click on `SAVE` button.

![Enable Logs](https://dashboard.eztovrfy.com/public/Docs/logs/enable-logs.png)

*3* Once the logs are enabled, all events are recorded in the database, making them accessible for review.

<br/>
