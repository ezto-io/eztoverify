##### How to setup Retarus

###### Prerequisites 
* You must have an [Retarus](https://www.retarus.com/) account.

###### Configure Retarus 

1. Log in to your [Retarus](https://www.retarus.com/) account. 

2. After Logged in, Setup a Credentials for the project you created in retarus.

3. Once sender Number is successfully configured, then copy the `SMS Gateway Url`, `Username` and `Password` into the dashboard settings
