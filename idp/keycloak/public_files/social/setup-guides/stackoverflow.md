###### Stack Overflow Configuration Instructions
Users will log in to your app using their Stack Overflow profile. 

###### How to setup Connection

###### Prerequisites 
* Create a Stack Overflow account before you begin.  

###### Create a Stack Overflow project for your app 
Please follow the below steps to complete the setup 

1. To enable Stack Overflow login, you must first complete a series of tasks. 

2. To use Stack Overflow for login, you must first create an OAuth application on `StackApps`. 

3. Log in to your Stack Apps account after registering your app. 

4. Click Register your Application after entering the application name and the OAuth Domain Name of your app.  

5. Fill in the blanks with whatever you like for the remaining things. 

6. Finally, you must copy the `client ID & secret`, and key from this page and paste them into the QlikVerify.  

7. Return to QlikVerify and indicate the things you want.
