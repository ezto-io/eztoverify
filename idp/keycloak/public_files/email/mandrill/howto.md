##### How to setup Mandrill

###### Prerequisites 
* You must have an [Mandrill](https://mandrill.com/) account.

###### Configure Mandrill 

1. Log in to your [Mandrill](https://mandrill.com/) account. 

2. Mandrill is a paid Mailchimp add-on.Add Mandrill to your Mailchimp account.

3. After Logged in, Configure your domain for email.

4. Create a new API in the dashboard.

5. Enable Email Messaging.

6. Now, Copy the `API Key` from Mandrill dashboard and paste it in the settings

###### Note

>**The value of the `API Key` field can refer a value from an external vault. It will be not stored in our database**
