##### How to setup Email OTP Factor

###### Configure Email OTP Factor

###### Email Verification Expiry Duration
  `Expiry Duration` denotes the when the OTP expires.Set the duration like,for days 2d, for hours 2h, for minutes 2m, for secs 2s

###### OTP Length
  `OTP Length` denotes the how many characters is the OTP? Short means more user friendly as it is less the user has to type. More means more security.

###### Email Sender
  `Email Sender` is used to choose the Email Sender to send the verification e-mail.Please ensure that the selected sender is configured correctly.
